Delete your Rallyd account

Rallyd Ltd · Account and associated-data deletion

You can request deletion without installing Rallyd or signing in to this website.

Request deletion by email

Email a Rallyd account-deletion request to carl@rallyd.app. Include your Rallyd handle, if known, and the email address and sign-in provider associated with your account. If possible, send the request from that email address.

The link opens an email draft. Your request is sent only when you send the email. If no email app opens, copy carl@rallyd.app into your email service and use the subject “Rallyd account deletion request”.

We verify account ownership before deleting data. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, payment-card details or identity documents with your request.

Delete from the app

Open Rallyd → Settings → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. You may be asked to sign in again to confirm ownership.

What is removed

Private profile details including email and photo, your username claim, solo-owned matches, your own feed activity and social connections, Rallyd's Google Play purchase-verification records, and your sign-in account are removed. Deletion cannot be undone.

What remains

Shared matches and competitions remain for other players' history. A limited historical profile retains your account identifier, display name and original account-creation date, with a deleted-account flag. It does not retain your email, photo, preferences or username. Shared records and this historical profile have no automatic expiry. Other players' own comments and reactions may also remain. You can contact us to discuss removal of your name from historical records.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically stop subscription renewals. Use Google Play subscriptions for purchases billed by Google, or follow Apple's cancellation instructions for App Store purchases. You can request deletion regardless of whether you have already cancelled. The stores retain their own transaction records under their respective policies.

See our Privacy Policy for more about retained data and your rights.
