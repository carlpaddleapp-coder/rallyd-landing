Five sports, scored right

Every rule, the way it's really played.

Tennis

Games, sets and the 6-6 tiebreak (first to 7, win by 2). Advantage, Golden or Star Point deuce.

Padel

Doubles by default, Golden Point at deuce — sudden death, the way padel is really played.

Badminton

Rally scoring to 21, win by 2, with the hard cap at 30.

Squash

PARS to 11, win by 2, best of 5 games.

Pickleball

Two-server side-out with Server 1 / Server 2 tracking in doubles. Rally scoring optional.
